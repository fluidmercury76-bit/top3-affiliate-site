Editorial process

How we choose products

Three practical choices, with sources, model details and trade-offs you can check.

Start with the job

We look for useful differences between three products: who each option suits, the space or setup it needs, and the compromise that might rule it out. They are not automatically ranked first to third.

Check the exact model

We use manufacturer and supplier sources to check model codes, dimensions, capacities and included accessories. Regional versions and retailer bundles can differ. Sources and review dates appear in each guide.

Compare newer options

We consider new releases and replacements, but do not change a recommendation simply because a newer model exists. Availability, a useful new function or clearer product information can justify a change.

Be clear about testing

The current catalogue is research-based. We have not personally tested the products, measured their performance or established long-term reliability. Manufacturer battery, capacity and performance figures retain their stated conditions.

Pictures and retailer links

Product pictures come from manufacturer or supplier references. A picture may show accessories or a colour that differs from the selected offer. Most Amazon buttons open model searches; they do not guarantee an exact listing or current stock.

Review and correct

The catalogue was reviewed on 3 November 2026. If you find an error, send the page address and supporting source. Read our affiliate disclosure to see how the site earns.
