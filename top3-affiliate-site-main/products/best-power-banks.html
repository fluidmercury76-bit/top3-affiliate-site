How to choose power banks

Start with your normal use, not the longest specification list. Compare usable capacity, USB-C Power Delivery, per-port output, simultaneous output, size, airline limits and whether your laptop needs high-wattage charging. The best choice is the model that removes the compromises you notice every week, not the one with the most features on paper.

Running cost and ownership

There is little direct electricity cost, but conversion losses mean the labelled battery capacity is not the same as energy delivered to your device. Battery cycle life and replacement matter more. Prices, tariffs, consumables and stock change, so use current figures when you make the final comparison.

Common problems before you replace it

Slow charging can be caused by the cable, port negotiation or device limit. Use a cable rated for the required power, test one device at a time and stop using a swollen, damaged or unusually hot battery pack. If a product shows heat damage, swelling, exposed wiring or another safety fault, stop using it and follow the manufacturer’s support advice.
