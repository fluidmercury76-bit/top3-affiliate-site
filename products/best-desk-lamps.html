How to choose desk lamps

Which desk lamp is useful for working from home?

Sketch where your screen, notebook and keyboard sit. BenQ’s wide-coverage approach is relevant to a spread-out setup; Forså is a simpler reading-spot option. Dyson makes more sense if the same desk also becomes an evening reading or ambient-light area. We have not measured glare or illumination ourselves.

Check reflections before adding brightness

A brighter lamp is not automatically a more comfortable setup. Think about where its reflection would fall on your screen and whether your writing hand would cast a shadow. Leave room to reposition the lamp instead of wedging it between the monitor and a wall.

Compare the complete purchase

For a bulb-based lamp, include a compatible bulb in the price and check its published brightness and colour. For an integrated lamp, look up the manufacturer’s service arrangements. USB charging and an app are conveniences; decide whether they solve a real problem on your desk.
