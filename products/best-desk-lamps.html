How to choose desk lamps

Start with your normal use, not the longest specification list. Compare light spread, glare control, colour temperature, dimming, reach, stability and whether the lamp illuminates the whole task area rather than a bright spot. The best choice is the model that removes the compromises you notice every week, not the one with the most features on paper.

Running cost and ownership

LED desk lamps use relatively little electricity. The bigger ownership questions are integrated versus replaceable light sources, warranty and whether smart features depend on an app. Prices, tariffs, consumables and stock change, so use current figures when you make the final comparison.

Common problems before you replace it

Flicker or erratic dimming can come from the power supply or controls. Check connections and approved adapters; position the lamp to avoid screen reflections and direct glare into your eyes. If a product shows heat damage, swelling, exposed wiring or another safety fault, stop using it and follow the manufacturer’s support advice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the best desk lamp?

Start with how you will use it most often, then compare size, comfort, power or capacity, warranty, cleaning and the main trade offs. The best desk lamp is the one that fits your normal use, not just the one with the longest feature list.

Is the premium desk lamp always worth it?

No. Premium models are worth it when the extra performance, build quality or convenience will be used regularly. For occasional use, the best value pick is often the sharper buy.
