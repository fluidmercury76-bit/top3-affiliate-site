How to choose coffee machines

Which coffee machine fits a small kitchen?

Baristina has the narrowest published body here at 18 cm, but its 38 cm depth is greater than Sage’s listed 34 cm. JURA is deepest at 44.6 cm. Measure width, depth and overhead access separately; do not decide from width alone.

Use a paper rectangle to mark each footprint on your counter. Then add your cup, space for a moving handle and any milk accessory. Check the manufacturer’s installation clearance before buying.

Espresso, lungo or latte?

For black coffee, extra milk automation may be unnecessary. Baristina concentrates on espresso and lungo. Sage textures milk in a jug; JURA uses a milk pipe. Think about whether you want involvement in preparing a drink or a more automatic sequence.

How much does each coffee cost?

Use your own bean price and actual dose: bean cost per drink = price per kilogram × grams used ÷ 1,000. For illustration only, £20/kg beans at 15 g per drink cost 30p; at 18 g they cost 36p. At two drinks a day for 30 days, that is £18 or £21.60 in beans. These are example doses, not measurements of these machines.

Add milk, cleaning products, filters, electricity and wasted coffee. Divide annual maintenance spending by your annual drink count. Purchase price divided by the number of drinks you expect to make gives a separate equipment cost per drink; it is not a prediction of machine lifespan.

Try a two-drink morning on paper

Write down your two usual drinks and the steps each machine would require. Include filling water, handling grounds, preparing milk and the cleanup afterwards. Then place cups and milk accessories on the intended worktop. This gives you a more realistic choice than comparing menu length alone.

Newer models do not remove the basic choice

Sage also offers Oracle Jet, while Philips now lists a Baristina Plus range. First choose whether you want a simple espresso routine, guided portafilter preparation or automatic milk drinks. Only compare a newer model after identifying which extra step or function it would improve for you.

Which of these coffee machines is smallest?

Philips Baristina is narrowest at 18 cm, but Sage has the shallower listed body at 34 cm. Check width, depth, height and access space together.

Does Philips Baristina make lattes?

The BAR300 prepares espresso and lungo. Milk foam needs a separate frother. Check whether a BAR303 bundle includes one; it is not the same as automatic milk dispensing.
