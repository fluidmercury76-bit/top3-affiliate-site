How to choose garden tools

Which garden tool should you buy first?

Write down the task you repeatedly postpone. For grass around edges, investigate the STIHL. For live stems, look at the Fiskars and its capacity. For several hand-tool jobs, choose the appropriate Wolf-Garten heads. Buying all three makes sense only if you actually have all three types of work.

Compare complete kits

Make a small shopping list with separate lines for tool, handle or battery, charger and any necessary accessories. A lower headline price for a bare tool may be poor value when the missing parts are added. Existing batteries help only when the manufacturer confirms system compatibility.

Fit and upkeep matter

Handle reach and hand fit affect whether a tool suits you, even when its nominal capacity looks right. Read the exact manual for use, protective equipment and maintenance. Confirm replacement cutting parts are available before committing to a system; do not infer parts compatibility from the brand alone.
