How to choose garden tools

Buying check 1

These three tools solve different jobs: the WOLF-Garten set prepares soil, the Fiskars pruner cuts live stems, and the STIHL trims grass edges. Choose the job first; buying all three is unnecessary for many small gardens.

Buying check 2

Check what comes in the box. The WOLF-Garten P512 includes a handle and two heads; STIHL FSA 50 listings may be bare tools or battery-and-charger kits. Compare complete usable kits rather than headline prices.
