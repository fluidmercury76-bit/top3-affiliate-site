How to choose shower heads

Start with your normal use, not the longest specification list. Compare your shower system, pressure, spray pattern, flow rate, hose connection, limescale resistance and water-saving goals. The best choice is the model that removes the compromises you notice every week, not the one with the most features on paper.

Running cost and ownership

Water and hot-water use dominate cost. A lower-flow handset can reduce both, but only if it still gives acceptable performance on your system. Prices, tariffs, consumables and stock change, so use current figures when you make the final comparison.

Common problems before you replace it

Poor spray usually means limescale or debris. Clean nozzles and filters as directed; if pressure is low throughout the house, a new handset will not fix the underlying supply problem. If a product shows heat damage, swelling, exposed wiring or another safety fault, stop using it and follow the manufacturer’s support advice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the best shower head?

Start with how you will use it most often, then compare size, comfort, power or capacity, warranty, cleaning and the main trade offs. The best shower head is the one that fits your normal use, not just the one with the longest feature list.

Is the premium shower head always worth it?

No. Premium models are worth it when the extra performance, build quality or convenience will be used regularly. For occasional use, the best value pick is often the sharper buy.
