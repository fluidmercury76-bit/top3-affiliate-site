How to choose shower heads

Will a new head fix a weak shower?

Do not assume so. First identify the shower model and check the manufacturer’s supported handsets. A product’s spray description does not establish the pressure available in your installation. If the supply or system is the problem, replacing the handset may leave that problem unchanged.

Electric shower compatibility comes first

Use the shower manufacturer’s guidance on replacements and flow restrictions. Do not add a restrictor or choose a handset just because the thread fits. If the approved accessory information is unclear, obtain confirmation for the exact shower and handset before buying.

Compare controls and care after compatibility

Once suitability is established, decide between Mira’s single spray and the selectable Pulsify settings, or investigate the Methven spray design. Check how each should be cleaned in your water conditions. We have not tested spray comfort, flow or water savings, and a claimed flow rate needs its stated test conditions.

Buying check 1

Check your shower's approved handset, water pressure and hose fitting before choosing by spray pattern. A replacement head cannot increase the pressure supplied by the plumbing. For electric showers, follow the shower manufacturer's compatibility guidance.

Buying check 2

The Pulsify 24101000 is the EcoSmart version, with a maximum stated flow of 6.9 litres per minute at 3 bar. Its product page gives differing minimum-pressure figures, so confirm suitability with hansgrohe if your pressure is marginal. Methven specifies high or mains pressure above 100kPa for the Aio handset.
