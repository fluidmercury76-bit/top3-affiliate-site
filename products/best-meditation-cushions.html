How to choose meditation cushions

Buying check 1

Compare height and shape before fabric colour. The round chambray cushion is 14 cm high, while the hemp zafu and crescent option are listed at 18 cm. Those are uncompressed dimensions; the seat changes when filled differently or sat on.

Buying check 2

Adjustable buckwheat filling lets you change firmness, but it cannot guarantee a comfortable posture. Try a shorter session first and change position if you become uncomfortable.

Buying check 3

Care differs substantially. The hemp zafu is spot-clean only and has no removable cover or separate liner. The chambray has a removable washable cover; check the specific Yoga Studio care label rather than assuming all buckwheat cushions can go in the wash.
