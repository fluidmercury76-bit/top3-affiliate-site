Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the best meditation cushion?

Start with how you will use it most often, then compare size, comfort, power or capacity, warranty, cleaning and the main trade offs. The best meditation cushion is the one that fits your normal use, not just the one with the longest feature list.

Is the premium meditation cushion always worth it?

No. Premium models are worth it when the extra performance, build quality or convenience will be used regularly. For occasional use, the best value pick is often the sharper buy.
