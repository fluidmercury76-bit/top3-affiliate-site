How to choose meditation cushions

Seat height: start with fit

The quoted dimensions describe the product, not a guaranteed sitting height under your weight. Compare a cushion’s shape with your usual sitting position. Adjustable filling gives you a way to change the seat rather than commit to its original volume.

Buckwheat or kapok?

For these two Yogamatters models, buckwheat can be removed to tune the filling. Do not assume a kapok cushion offers the same access: check the individual product’s construction. Choose by the adjustment and care features you will use, rather than the filling name alone.

Cleaning can decide the purchase

If easy washing matters most, the cotton option has a clear advantage over the spot-clean hemp model. Always follow the care label for the selected variant; a washable cover does not mean the whole filled cushion can go into a washing machine.

Try the seat without forcing a posture

You do not have to sit on the floor to meditate. A comfortable chair is another option. If a seat feels wrong, change your position rather than treating discomfort as something the cushion should make you tolerate.

Final verdict

The most useful choice here is between a taller hemp seat and a lower cotton seat with easier cover cleaning. Leewadee remains a conditional kapok alternative: verify the exact standalone listing before buying. Check stock and returns so you can choose for your own sitting routine.

Frequently asked questions

Which meditation cushion height should I choose?

Compare the quoted height with a seat you already find comfortable. The two Yogamatters cushions have different starting heights, but both allow filling adjustments. Published dimensions do not guarantee the same height when you sit on the cushion.

Can I wash a meditation cushion?

Check the care label for your exact model. A removable washable cover and a washable filled cushion are different things. Keep filling dry unless the manufacturer explicitly says otherwise.

What is the difference between a zafu and a zabuton?

A zafu is a raised meditation seat. A zabuton is the flatter mat placed underneath. Check whether a listing includes one cushion or a complete set.
