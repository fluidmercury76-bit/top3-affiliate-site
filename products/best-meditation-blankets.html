How to choose meditation blankets

Start with fabric feel

For draping over bare arms or your neck, look for a soft finish and comfortable seams. Pre-washed muslin is worth considering if you want a light cotton layer. Fabric composition alone cannot guarantee how a blanket will feel against your skin.

Measure while seated

Try an existing blanket around your shoulders and across your lap. Compare the width and length with the listed dimensions. A compact throw is easier to carry, while a longer one offers more fabric to cover your legs.

Keep decoration comfortable

A plain body with a subtle fringe gives a quiet appearance. Check whether the edging sits where your hands or feet rest. Loosely woven fabrics and tassels can catch, so follow the care instructions and inspect the finish when it arrives.

A wrap or a folding prop?

A muslin throw provides a light covering. A larger yoga blanket may be more useful when folded for support, but will feel different to wear. For a raised seat, compare our meditation cushions; for a separate floor surface, see our yoga mat comparison.

Buying check 1

For a light meditation cover, start with a plain cotton muslin throw that is large enough for your shoulders or legs. The two muslin options offer restrained fringe detail without the deliberate weight of a weighted blanket.

Buying check 2

Texture is personal: no blanket can be promised itch-free for everyone. Check the fibre label, washing instructions and returns terms before washing or using it.

Buying check 3

The Yogamatters blanket is a different choice: at 1.2 kg and 230 × 150 cm it offers more material for folding as a prop. Its brushed finish can shed, so it is less suitable if your priority is a small, airy, low-lint cover.
