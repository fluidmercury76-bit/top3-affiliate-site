Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the best meditation blanket?

Start with how you will use it most often, then compare size, comfort, power or capacity, warranty, cleaning and the main trade offs. The best meditation blanket is the one that fits your normal use, not just the one with the longest feature list.

Is the premium meditation blanket always worth it?

No. Premium models are worth it when the extra performance, build quality or convenience will be used regularly. For occasional use, the best value pick is often the sharper buy.
