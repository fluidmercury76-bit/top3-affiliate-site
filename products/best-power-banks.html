How to choose power banks

What wattage does your laptop need?

Read the laptop’s USB-C charging requirements first. A high-wattage bank cannot make a device accept more power than it supports, and a barrel-plug laptop is not automatically compatible. Check the port, protocol and cable together. The CUKTECH and UGREEN headline totals are not single-port ratings.

Why capacity does not equal an exact number of phone charges

Do not simply divide the bank’s mAh by the phone’s mAh and promise that many full charges. Voltage conversion, losses and phone use during charging affect the result. Decide whether you need an occasional top-up or a longer reserve; we have not measured delivered energy for these products.

Remember the journey back to full

A bank also needs recharging. Check its supported input and whether the box contains an appropriate charger and cable. If you regularly charge two devices, read the power-sharing table for that precise port combination rather than looking only at the maximum output.
