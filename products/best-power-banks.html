How to choose power banks

Buying check 1

Match the output of one port to your device, rather than comparing the largest number on the box. CUKTECH 20 P23 supplies up to 140W from its main USB-C port. The UGREEN 75702 supplies up to 100W from each USB-C output and up to 200W shared across outputs. Neither shared-output figure means that much power goes to one laptop.

Buying check 2

The UGREEN 75702 adds two built-in USB-C cables, including a 68cm retractable cable, plus USB-C and USB-A sockets. That reduces loose cables, but integrated cables can wear and its 631g weight suits a bag rather than a pocket. Anker's 5,000mAh Nano is the smaller magnetic phone option.

Buying check 3

Battery capacity is measured at the internal cells; conversion losses mean the full printed mAh figure does not reach your phone. Check your device's charging protocol and cable requirements, and consult your airline's current battery rules before travelling.
