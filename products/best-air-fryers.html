How to choose air fryers

How big an air fryer do you need?

Start with a typical meal rather than a household-size label. Check the room for your usual pieces of food, whether you need separate cooking temperatures and whether you accept batches. Two drawers can separate foods, but splitting a total capacity also limits the space in each drawer.

Will it fit below the kitchen cupboards?

Mark the body footprint and then account for opening, loading and the manufacturer’s ventilation clearances. Double Stack’s listed body is 28 cm wide, 47 cm deep and 38.5 cm high. CRISPi needs somewhere to put its containers; those working areas are not captured by one appliance measurement.

Compare cleanup as part of the meal

List the basket or container, plates, racks and lids you would use for a normal dinner. Read which parts are dishwasher safe and how they should cool and be cleaned. We have not tested cooking evenness, cooking speed or washing effort, so programme counts are not performance scores.
