How to choose wireless earbuds

What matters for calls across a laptop and phone?

List the devices you use on a normal day and check how switching works. Technics’ three-device multipoint is relevant to that routine. We have not tested microphone quality or switching reliability, so a connectivity feature should not be read as a guarantee of better calls.

Read the battery footnotes

Separate listening time in the earbuds from extra charge stored in the case. Also compare the listening modes used for the claim. Bose quotes up to six hours with Immersive Audio off and four with it on; neither figure should be compared with another brand’s case-total number.

Fit deserves as much attention as features

Check the supplied tip sizes, wearing instructions and the seller’s hygiene and return conditions before ordering. We have not tested comfort or noise-cancellation performance. A long features list cannot establish whether the earbuds will stay comfortable in your own ears through a commute or a call.

Buying check 1

Fit comes before extra features: the same earbud can seal well for one person and poorly for another. Choose Technics AZ100 for three-device multipoint, Nothing Ear (3a) for a simpler alternative with active noise cancellation, or Bose Ultra second generation if its noise-cancelling features and fit suit you. These are researched comparisons, not a claim that we tested their sound ourselves.

Buying check 2

Compare battery figures using the same settings. Bose quotes up to six hours with Immersive Audio off and four with it on. A total that includes recharging from a case is not the same as continuous listening from the earbuds. IPX4 ratings cover splashes rather than swimming.

Frequently asked questions

Does multipoint play audio from three devices at once?

No. Technics’ three-device multipoint refers to connected devices and switching, not a promise to mix three simultaneous audio streams.

Why does Bose list different listening times?

Its specifications state up to six hours with Immersive Audio off and up to four with it on. Settings change the relevant battery claim.
