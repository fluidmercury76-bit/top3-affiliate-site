How to choose blenders

Personal blender or a jug?

For a single breakfast drink, compare the cup-based Nutribullet routine with pouring from and washing a jug. For multiple servings, check the usable amount in one batch and whether you are happy repeating a cycle. A bigger jug may be unnecessary if you normally make one small portion.

Do watts tell you which makes the smoothest drink?

No. We have not measured texture, noise or blending time. Container design, ingredients and preparation also matter. Treat published power as an electrical specification and programme lists as controls, rather than converting them into unsupported performance rankings.

Check your actual recipe and storage space

Read the manual for the exact model before preparing hot mixtures, frozen ingredients or thick recipes; do not transfer permissions between different container formats. Check maximum-fill guidance and where you will store the jug, cups and lids. Also look for replacement seals and containers before committing to a setup.
