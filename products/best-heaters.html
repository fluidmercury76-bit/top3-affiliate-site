How to choose heaters

Compare running costs at the same electricity rate

At an illustrative 25p/kWh, continuous use at the listed maximum power costs 37.5p per hour for a 1.5 kW heater, 50p for 2 kW and about 56.3p for 2.25 kW. Thermostats can cycle the heat off, so this is a full-power comparison, not a forecast of the bill. Use your own tariff.

Why HF1 replaces AM09 here

The reason is its connected controls and scheduling, not an assumption that newer always heats better. If you will not use those functions, compare the simpler Dimplex and Dreo formats first. HF1’s fan mode can provide airflow in warmer weather but is not air conditioning.
