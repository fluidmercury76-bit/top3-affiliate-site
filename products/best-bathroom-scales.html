How to choose bathroom scales

Which app do you want to keep using?

Look at the app before choosing the scale. Consider whether you can easily find the history you care about, which features require an account and whether any optional services have charges. If you only want a weight readout, ask whether you need a connected model at all.

Plan household profiles and privacy

Check how each person creates an account and how readings are assigned. Do not assume every metric is visible to every household member, or that privacy settings are identical across brands. Read the current account and data-sharing controls before linking another service.

What can this comparison say about accuracy?

We have not tested these scales against reference equipment. Body-composition readouts are estimates and should not be treated as a diagnosis. Follow the exact manual for positioning, measurement conditions and any restrictions on using its body-composition functions; compare trends cautiously rather than treating one reading as a verdict.

Buying check 1

Choose a scale around the app you will actually use. Body Smart includes core measurements and trends without a Withings+ subscription; Garmin Index S2 is most useful if you already use Garmin Connect. Body-composition figures are estimates, so compare trends measured under similar conditions rather than treating a daily change as a precise measurement of fat or muscle.
