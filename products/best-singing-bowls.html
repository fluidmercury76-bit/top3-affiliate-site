How to choose singing bowls

Buying check 1

Choose a bowl for its size, sound and ease of use. Small bowls take less storage space, while the much larger Peter Hess Universal is a different handling experience. A product name alone does not tell you how loud or sustained its sound will be.

Buying check 2

Listen to a recording of the exact model where available, but treat it as a guide rather than a promise. Your room, striker and playing technique affect the sound, and handmade bowls can vary.

Buying check 3

Check the included accessories. A starter set may include a cushion and striker; a larger bowl may require those separately. We do not claim that a particular frequency heals conditions or that these products have been personally sound-tested.
