How to choose fans

A newer Meaco is not automatically easier to buy

Sefte 10 and Sefte Pro 10 add newer designs, and the Pro adds battery and app features. At review, Meaco marked all three table models, including 1056, out of stock. Check actual retailer availability and delivery dates before deciding. The 1056 remains relevant if you prefer mains operation and no app.

Battery hours need their conditions

Shark’s 24-hour maximum is for speed one without oscillation. Higher speeds and oscillation change the useful runtime. Duux’s standard Flex 2 Smart package excludes its optional battery. Choose the mains or battery routine first, then compare what the box actually contains.
