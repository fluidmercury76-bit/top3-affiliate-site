How to choose electric toothbrushes

What are you paying for beyond a timer?

The useful distinction here is how much adjustment and feedback you want. Philips provides several modes and intensities; Ordo offers a simpler timed routine; Oral-B adds its iOsense setup. These are feature comparisons, not clinical tests of cleaning performance.

Work out the cost of replacement heads

Use the cost per compatible head, not the price of a multipack alone. Multiply by the number you expect to use following the manufacturer’s replacement guidance, and include every person sharing the handle. Check compatibility on the exact product rather than assuming every head from one brand fits.

Plan for charging and travel

Check which cable, plug, base and case the selected offer contains. A storage case and a charging case are different things. Think about where you will put the charger and whether any app features require a phone you intend to keep using. Avoid paying for a bundle accessory you will leave in a drawer.
