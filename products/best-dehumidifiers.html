How to choose dehumidifiers

Start with your normal use, not the longest specification list. Compare extraction rate, room temperature, tank size, humidistat, laundry mode, noise, drainage options and room size. The best choice is the model that removes the compromises you notice every week, not the one with the most features on paper.

Running cost and ownership

Compressor and desiccant models behave differently. Running cost depends on wattage, hours used and how quickly the unit reaches the target humidity, so compare real duty cycle rather than headline power alone. Prices, tariffs, consumables and stock change, so use current figures when you make the final comparison.

Common problems before you replace it

Low extraction may be normal in cool or already-dry rooms. Check filters, airflow clearance, target humidity and drain setup; frost, unusual noise or repeated error codes need the manufacturer troubleshooting steps. If a product shows heat damage, swelling, exposed wiring or another safety fault, stop using it and follow the manufacturer’s support advice.
