How to choose patio heaters

Start with your normal use, not the longest specification list. Compare heat direction, mounting position, weather rating, power level, cable route and whether radiant heat reaches seated people rather than empty air. The best choice is the model that removes the compromises you notice every week, not the one with the most features on paper.

Running cost and ownership

Electric patio heaters can draw 1.5 to 2.5kW or more. Running cost is simply power multiplied by time and your tariff, so timers and targeted radiant heat matter. Prices, tariffs, consumables and stock change, so use current figures when you make the final comparison.

Common problems before you replace it

Weak heating is often a placement problem. Wind and distance reduce comfort quickly; keep the unit within its approved clearances and never improvise indoor use for an outdoor heater. If a product shows heat damage, swelling, exposed wiring or another safety fault, stop using it and follow the manufacturer’s support advice.
