How to choose bluetooth speakers

Portable speaker or a room-to-garden speaker?

Picture the most common journey. Carrying a speaker in a bag is a different requirement from moving it from kitchen to patio. Compare physical dimensions and the charging setup with that journey. Sonos is particularly relevant if you also want its home Wi-Fi role.

Can you compare 12-hour and 24-hour claims directly?

Only with matching test conditions, which we have not independently established here. Volume, settings and use affect battery life. Treat the published maxima as manufacturer claims and leave a margin for your own listening instead of planning a trip to the last advertised hour.

Connectivity and weather checks

Decide whether Bluetooth alone is enough or whether home Wi-Fi features justify the extra setup. Follow each product’s water-exposure and charging instructions; a protection rating does not make every outdoor use appropriate. We have not measured loudness, bass, clarity or weather durability.
