How to choose yoga mats

Is a thicker yoga mat always better?

These standard mats range from 4 to 5 mm. That small thickness difference does not establish which feels best to you: material, floor and practice matter too. If kneeling comfort is your priority, compare the return terms and any separate padding you already use instead of treating millimetres as a comfort score.

Which mat is easier to take to class?

Jade’s listed 1.8 kg is 580 g lighter than lululemon’s 2.38 kg; Manduka’s UK standard is between them at 2.1 kg. Add the strap or bag and everything else you carry. Check length as well: the lighter Jade is 173 cm long, compared with 180 cm for the two standard alternatives.

Grip claims need context

We have not measured wet or dry grip. Manduka recommends a towel for sweaty sessions, while lululemon describes a moisture-absorbing top layer. Think about your usual practice rather than interpreting either description as proof of superior grip. Follow each brand’s care instructions; a cleaner approved for one surface may not suit another.

No. lululemon and Jade use natural rubber and carry latex-related advice. Manduka describes PROlite as PVC and latex-free. Check the exact product materials.
