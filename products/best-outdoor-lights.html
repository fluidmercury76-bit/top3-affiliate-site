How to choose outdoor lights

Buying check 1

Start with the area you want to light. An up-and-down wall light emphasises brickwork and entrances; a low-level spotlight picks out a plant or feature. Neither automatically replaces a broad security floodlight.

Buying check 2

Hue Appear needs a mains connection and Hue Bridge. Lily uses the Hue LowVolt system, but this selected extension light excludes the power supply and also needs a Bridge. Include those extras in the first-installation cost.

Buying check 3

Govee H7076 is the Outdoor UpDown Wall Light, distinct from H7075. Its app-controlled colour effects and separately paired motion-sensor option suit decorative lighting. Check the exact model, pack quantity and installation instructions rather than transferring specifications between similarly named Govee lights.
