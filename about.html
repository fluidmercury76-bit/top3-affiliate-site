About Top 3 Choices

Top 3 Choices is run by Tim McGregor. The aim is simple: help you compare three practical options without getting lost in a long list of similar products.

What the site offers

We explain the differences that can change a buying decision: the exact model, what comes in the box, how it fits into a routine and the compromises to consider. Our guides cover the kitchen, bathroom, home comfort, technology, outdoor living and quieter moments at home.

Research, clearly labelled

The current guides are based on manufacturer and supplier information, not our own laboratory or hands-on testing. Recommendations are editorial judgements about the published differences. We do not invent review scores, personal experience or performance results.

Why three choices?

Three options keep the comparison manageable. They can serve different jobs, formats or budgets. We do not force every guide into an overall, value and premium ranking.

Keeping the choices useful

The full product catalogue was reviewed on 3 November 2026. New products are considered when they offer a useful difference; an older model can remain a sensible choice. Prices, stock and retailer bundles can change after a review.
