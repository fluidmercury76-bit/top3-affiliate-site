Convenience still includes maintenance

Decide who in the household will complete the milk-system clean. JURA specifies daily cleaning when milk has been prepared. Convenient drink preparation is useful only if the follow-up routine works for you too.

Measure depth before buying

Check how far the machine would project towards the worktop edge. Leave usable space for cups and the milk arrangement, and make sure you can reach the water and beans without a difficult daily lift.

J10 is not J10 twin

These notes cover the J10, using JURA’s Piano Black 15664 listing. Do not apply another model’s grinder setup, dimensions or accessories to this one. Confirm the model and included milk accessories with the seller.
