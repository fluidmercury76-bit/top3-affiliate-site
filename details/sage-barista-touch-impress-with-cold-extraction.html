Would you enjoy the routine?

Imagine making two drinks before leaving for work. Are you happy moving a portafilter and preparing a jug of milk, or would you prefer a machine that dispenses the finished drink? That distinction is more useful here than choosing by screen size.

Check the exact version

This comparison covers the Cold Extraction model, SES882/BES882. Ask the seller to confirm the model code if the title is shortened. The supplied accessories and regional warranty also deserve a check.

Plan space around the machine

Mark its footprint on the counter, then leave working room in front for your cup and handle. Check overhead access before putting it below a cupboard. Body dimensions do not include your working area.
