Match the model, not just the brand

Silentnight sells several weighted blankets. This page refers to the Wellbeing 6.8 kg model. Do not transfer its dimensions or care instructions to a cooling model, a 9 kg version or a children’s blanket.

Plan how you will handle it

Before ordering, consider carrying it between rooms and storing it when you finish. Ask the seller for the complete fabric, filling and care label if those details are missing. A machine-washable label alone does not confirm that your own machine can handle the load.
