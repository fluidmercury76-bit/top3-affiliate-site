Is the separate cover useful to you?

A removable cover is a practical reason to shortlist this model if you expect frequent surface cleaning. It does not make the weighted inner washable. Compare how easily you can remove and refit the cover before deciding whether the extra maintenance suits you.

Check the listing contents

For a marketplace or used listing, confirm that the offer is the complete blanket with its cover. Compare the condition and return terms as well as the product name. A replacement cover alone is a different purchase.
