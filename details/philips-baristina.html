Small width does not mean no workspace

Its narrow body is attractive for a shared worktop. Before ordering, check where you will put your cup, empty the coffee grounds and store a frother if you use one. A photograph can make the complete setup look smaller than it is.

Choose your usual drink first

If your normal order is espresso or lungo, the limited menu may be an advantage. If you want a latte prepared in one sequence, compare the JURA instead. A separate frother adds another vessel to clean and store.

Check the bundle

Philips distinguishes the BAR300 machine from BAR303 offers with a milk frother. Compare the actual box contents and model number before treating two retailer prices as equivalent.
